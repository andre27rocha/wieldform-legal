Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains what data the WIELDFORM game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account identifiers. The game signs you in anonymously through Unity Gaming Services and gives you a Player ID. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your player profile. Your in-game name, tag and chosen avatar.
  • Game progress. Your save (stages, equipment, currencies, talents and similar) is stored on Unity Gaming Services servers so that it is kept safe and can be checked for cheating.
  • Messages, when chat is available: the messages you send, kept so they can be shown to other players and reviewed when reported.
  • Gameplay analytics, only if you agree: how long and how often you play, how far you progress, and where players stop. We use this to balance the game. You can refuse and still play.
  • Advertising data. Rewarded ads are served by Unity Ads, which may collect a device advertising identifier and ad interaction data under its own privacy policy.

Who processes it

Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services, Unity Analytics, Unity Ads) processes this data for us. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and saved progress are removed.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data at any time: Settings › Delete account (you confirm twice). If you cannot open the game, ask via Support.
  • Analytics are only collected after you agree, and you can withdraw that choice in Settings.
  • Ads: you can limit ad tracking in your device settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: see Support.
