Support

To report a problem, ask a question or request help with your account, open an issue at github.com/andre27rocha/wieldform-legal/issues.

Please include your Player ID (Settings › Player ID › Copy) and a short description of what happened. Do not post passwords or payment details in an issue: issues are public.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all of its data from inside the game: Settings › Delete account. The game asks you to confirm twice. Deletion removes your account and your saved progress from our servers and from your device, and cannot be undone.

If you can no longer open the game and want your data deleted, open an issue with your Player ID and we will delete it for you.
